## Notion workflow skills

Each workflow below is a separate bundle/plugin so package access or a one-time
purchase can grant an individual item. The descriptions use client-selected
Notion content and keep writes and external messages under customer control.

| Bundle | First-value outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Morning Brief | A prioritized daily brief from selected work, relationship, opportunity, calendar, and communication signals. |
| Weekly Prep | A focused plan that reconciles completed, stalled, waiting, and new work. |
| Meeting Processor | Source-linked decisions, commitments, follow-ups, and relationship or opportunity changes from selected meetings. |
| Networking Follow-up | A low-noise view of promised follow-ups with customer-reviewable message drafts. |
| Opportunity Intelligence | An evidence-based opportunity assessment with fit, unknowns, changes, and next steps. |
| Coaching Progress Review | A source-grounded review of coaching progress, decisions, friction, and proposed actions. |
| Career Transition Brief | Company-first preparation with dated public research and source-backed customer context. |
| Coaching Transcript Collector | An opt-in archive proposal for selected coaching transcripts with duplicate and privacy checks. |

These are customer-invoked workflow skills, not active Notion schedules or
event watchers. They propose work in ChatGPT and require customer approval for
durable changes. Standalone workflow skills use the owner-approved USD 19
one-time price; package inclusion carries no extra charge. Stripe catalog
identifiers and customer checkout remain outside this authoring repository.
