# Representative bundle pilot kits

P17 supplies one reusable first-run scenario for each of the six bundles. Every
kit includes a realistic fictional source packet, setup instructions that stay
outside the timer, an unaided task script, observable completion evidence, an
expert rubric, safety checks, and common advancement thresholds.

## What the kits prove

A synthetic run proves that the scenario and measurement flow can be rehearsed
safely. It does not prove client value. Only attempts explicitly marked
`authorized_representative` contribute to an advancement decision, and the
evaluator requires at least three attempts, including two unaided attempts.

An `advance_to_controlled_beta` result means the bounded scenario met its local
thresholds. It is not a production-readiness, general-market, causality, or
indispensability claim. Installed-host, deployed operations, support, commercial,
provider, and client authorization gates remain independent.

## Comparable protocol

1. Choose a participant whose current work matches the kit's participant-fit
   description. Obtain authorization before using any real material.
2. Prepare the source packet and environment before timing. The checked-in packet
   is fictional and may be used for rehearsal; never present it as client data.
3. Record the participant's usual-process baseline before opening the target
   workspace. Do not include setup time in the timed attempt.
4. Start the native private value check, then give only the kit's timed objective.
   Do not coach navigation or interpretation. Count every facilitator action.
5. Stop when the observable first outcome is reached or when a defined
   abandonment reason applies. Record the private value-check result.
6. Capture only structured issue codes in the portable run record. Do not copy
   prompts, sources, outputs, names, notes, or client work into measurement data.
7. Assess all attempts together. Synthetic rehearsals are reported separately and
   excluded from the representative decision.

## Shared advancement threshold

Each bundle requires at least three authorized representative attempts and two
unaided attempts. At least two thirds must complete, reach the declared outcome,
and meet the manifest's time target. Median usefulness, trust, and actionability
must each be at least 4/5; median corrections must be no more than two; every
completed attempt must pass the evidence, provenance, and mutation-control gates;
and no critical safety issue may occur.

The threshold is intentionally a controlled-beta gate, not a universal product
claim. A failed or abandoned run is evidence to improve the bundle, not something
to omit from the cohort.

## Scenario catalog

| Bundle | Scenario | Core proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executive | Find the three moves that deserve attention | Prioritization, exact source links, and task-metadata-only cross-bundle restraint |
| Writer & Editor | Turn an unfinished resource into a publishable recommendation | Voice preservation, combined review coverage, provenance, and approval-only publishing |
| Ministry | Build a source-layered teaching brief | Explicit research layers, honest citations, prior-work status, and belief restraint |
| Nonprofit Founder | Turn a founder idea into owned next moves | Sequencing, source-first regulatory questions, outreach control, and clinical separation |
| Investor | Decide whether new evidence changes a thesis | Fact/interpretation separation, counterevidence, freshness, invalidation, and no trading |
| Workspace Experience | Open once and know where to begin | Entitlement-derived composition, prioritized attention, exact navigation, and confirmed layout |

The executable definitions and evaluator live in
[`bundles/pilot-kit.ts`](../../bundles/pilot-kit.ts). The client-facing host may
render the same definitions, but it must not let static fixture data become an
authority source or silently seed a user's real workspace.
