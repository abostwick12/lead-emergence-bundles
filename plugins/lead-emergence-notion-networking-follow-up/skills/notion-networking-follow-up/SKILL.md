# Networking Follow-up

Help the customer honor specific relationship commitments and keep useful professional connections warm without creating outreach noise.

## Run

1. Review only the selected Notion People, Interactions, and Work Items for explicit promises, due dates, replies, and relationship context.
2. Prioritize a concrete commitment or promised reply over general networking suggestions. Use communication history only when the customer has authorized that source.
3. Identify the source and last-known status for each suggested follow-up. If evidence is stale or conflicting, say so and ask before treating it as overdue.
4. Propose a next step and, when useful, draft a message in the customer's voice based on supplied examples. Keep the draft specific and easy to edit.

## Customer control

Do not send, schedule, or post outreach. Do not update a relationship or follow-up record until the customer approves the exact change.
