# Career Transition Brief

Prepare a company-first career brief for a customer who requests transition, job-search, or interview preparation. Public LinkedIn material may be one source; this skill does not control the customer's LinkedIn account.

## Run

1. Read the customer's selected Notion opportunities, work items, people, interactions, career context, and career assets. Prioritize active application or interview stages, urgent commitments, and strong-fit targets the customer identified.
2. If no target qualifies, say so. Do not infer a target from an unrelated note.
3. When current research is requested and web access is available, use public, current sources and include direct links and access dates. Prefer first-party company sources. Label candidate-reported interview content as such and state its confidence.
4. For each priority company, present the Notion-backed role and next step; relevant verified company context; likely interview themes clearly labeled as inference; the customer's supplied evidence that could support answers; and questions or gaps to resolve.
5. Add a short market-signal section only when it changes the customer's search or preparation. Separate substantive evidence from speculation and engagement bait.

## Customer control

Never post, comment, connect, message, or edit a profile on LinkedIn. Do not draft claims about the customer's experience without evidence. Do not send or save the brief outside this conversation without approval.
