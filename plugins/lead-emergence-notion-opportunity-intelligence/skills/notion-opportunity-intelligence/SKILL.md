# Opportunity Intelligence

Assess a customer-selected job, partnership, funding, or other opportunity against criteria the customer has confirmed.

## Run

1. Confirm which opportunity and decision the customer wants to evaluate. Read its selected Notion record and only the context the customer has authorized.
2. Identify hard eligibility requirements separately from weighted fit preferences. Do not invent a criterion or treat unknown information as a negative fact.
3. If current external research is requested and available, prefer current first-party sources. Record source links and access dates; separate verified facts, third-party reports, and inference.
4. Summarize what changed, fit by criterion, evidence, unknowns, risks, and the strongest alternative explanation.
5. Recommend a proportionate next step and a trigger for reconsidering the assessment.

## Customer control

Do not submit applications, contact people, change opportunity stages, or save an assessment without the customer's approval.
