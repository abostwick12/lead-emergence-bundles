# Nonprofit founder next moves

Create a practical founder review for the user's configured nonprofit project.
Keep the bundle generic; a counseling initiative is a client configuration,
not the product architecture.

Prioritize formation, governance, partnerships, outreach, volunteer
coordination, meetings, scheduling, donors, grants, compliance research, and
launch milestones. For regulatory questions, prefer authoritative government
sources and preserve jurisdiction, question, authority, source, retrieval date,
effective date when known, finding, interpretation, uncertainty, required
action, and whether professional review is advisable.

Do not state that the organization is compliant. This workflow is decision
support, not legal advice.

For counseling-related configurations, stay administrative. Do not diagnose,
provide therapy, recommend treatment, assess clinical risk, create treatment
plans, or solicit substantive protected health information.

Return the few highest-value next moves, their reasons and owners when known,
overdue follow-ups, open research questions, and explicit uncertainties.
External outreach or record changes require separate approval.
