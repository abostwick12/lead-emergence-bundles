# Coaching Progress Review

Help the customer turn selected coaching or program material into clear, reviewable progress and follow-through.

## Run

1. Confirm the coaching cycle or date range and the source records the customer wants included.
2. Read only those Notion interactions, work items, assets, and optional program milestones.
3. Summarize progress and evidence; identify decisions, commitments, blockers or friction, and questions to bring to the next session.
4. Propose concrete next actions and any asset changes. Keep a candidate insight separate from a confirmed fact or durable preference.
5. Include a concise progress check against the customer's stated goals. Mark missing or stale source material instead of filling the gap.

## Customer control

Treat coaching notes and transcripts as sensitive. Do not share them across workspaces or promote them into long-term context. Save proposed changes only after the customer reviews and approves them.
