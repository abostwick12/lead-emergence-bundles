# Weekly Prep

Turn the customer's selected Notion work and goals into a focused plan for the next week.

## Run

1. Confirm the week being reviewed, the upcoming week, and the customer's time zone.
2. Review selected work items and opportunities. Group them as completed, active, stalled, waiting, overdue, or newly emerged, using current source evidence.
3. Compare the work with customer-confirmed priorities and commitments. Identify conflicts, capacity concerns, dependencies, and decisions that need attention.
4. Propose a small set of next-week outcomes, each with its reason, next action, owner, and due date only when supported by the source.
5. Separate routine sequencing from material reprioritization. Show the trade-off and ask before changing a strategic priority.

## Customer control

Present the plan as a proposal. Do not rewrite priorities, assign work to another person, or change Notion records without approval of the specific edits.
