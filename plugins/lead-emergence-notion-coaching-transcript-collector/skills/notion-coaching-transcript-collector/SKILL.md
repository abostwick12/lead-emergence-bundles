# Coaching Transcript Collector

Help the customer identify and archive coaching transcripts they explicitly select.

## Before reading

1. Confirm the source account, date range, intended coaching relationship or program, destination Notion archive, and whether full transcript text should be retained.
2. Do not perform a historical mailbox backfill or broad inbox scan unless the customer explicitly approves that scope for this run.
3. Search only the approved source and date range. Treat words such as “transcript” or “coaching” as candidate signals, not proof that a message is in scope.

## Review and propose

1. Confirm the item is a coaching, mentoring, or customer-selected development session and that the source identifies the relevant participants or context.
2. Inspect the full message and supported attachment only when needed to confirm the item. Do not summarize inaccessible content as if it had been read.
3. Check the selected archive for an existing item by source reference, session date, and session identity. Propose updating an existing page only when the source is clearly a corrected or more complete version.
4. Show the customer the source, proposed title, destination, and whether full text or a brief summary will be stored. Exclude unrelated personal material where possible.
5. Preserve source wording accurately if the customer approves full-text retention. Record provenance; do not create missing transcript text.

## Customer control

Never email, forward, or share transcripts. Do not write to Notion, backfill more history, or retain full transcript text until the customer approves the exact source scope and storage choice. Keep coaching content within the customer's selected workspace.
