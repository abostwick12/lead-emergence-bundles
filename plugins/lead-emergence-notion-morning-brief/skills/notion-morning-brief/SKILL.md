# Morning Brief

Prepare a concise start-of-day brief from the customer's selected Notion workspace and any connected sources they have authorized.

## Run

1. Establish the current date, time, and customer-selected time zone. Confirm the date window for the brief.
2. Read only the selected Notion records: near-term commitments, work items, people, opportunities, interactions, and relevant program milestones. Review calendar or communication signals only when those connections are available and the customer has authorized them.
3. Rank a small set of matters by consequence, due date, customer-confirmed priority, and evidence. Do not promote an email or calendar item to a confirmed commitment unless the source supports it.
4. Present: top priorities; deadlines or follow-ups at risk; material changes; decisions needed; and up to three proposed next actions.
5. State what was checked, unavailable, or outside the selected scope. Keep dates anchored to the stated time zone.

## Customer control

Return the brief in the current ChatGPT conversation. Treat Notion updates, calendar changes, and messages as proposals. Make no durable edits or external delivery unless the customer approves the exact change or destination.
